Ignorance

Misguided, mislead, mistaken, misgiven
The mind is a jail, the body, a prison
Unfounded, undone, unholy, unwritten
The taste of the apple, the skin that was bitten

The act to deceive, the moral to grieve
The daughter produced it, the son sowed the seed
The prophet, the grail, the serpent, the steed
If a man can induce it, the savior can bleed

And he did that indeed, and time washed it down
Erasing, eroding, embracing the ground
When it's hard to believe, shepherds stray from the sound
And abandon a fact when it lives to astound

The fact that the phoenix arises from ash
And the liar, the cheater, will burn with the grass
Until God, the defeater's deleted the last
Until dust turns to dust and the present to past
